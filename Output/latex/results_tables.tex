\label{tab:method-comparison}

\begin{tabular}{llrrrrr}
\toprule
Analysis & $n$ & Existing gap & Proposed gap & Change & 95\% CI & GEE $p$ \\
\midrule

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\par\medskip
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}
\footnotesize
Gaussian identity-link GEEs use working independence and race-clustered robust sandwich standard errors. Tests are two-sided Wald tests; intervals are robust Wald 95\% confidence intervals for the same mean contrasts. Matched winners use an intercept-only model for female-minus-male differences within race--age-group pairs; all finishers use a sex indicator with male as reference. $n$ counts pairs or runners, respectively; clusters are 216 races. Each pair or runner has equal weight. Differences are percentage points. Inference assumes independent races and conditions on the fitted standards. Change is the proposed gap minus the existing gap. Direct-comparison models use within-runner changes in age grade, paired across sex for matched winners.
\end{minipage}
\end{table}
